ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Education that helps students grow.

Arunima Vidya Mandir is an educational institution located in Bheemdatta-2, Kanchanpur, Nepal. The school is committed to creating a supportive and student-centered learning environment.

Along with academic learning, the school encourages communication, computer and digital literacy, creativity, critical thinking, discipline, confidence, and responsibility.
